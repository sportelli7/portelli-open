Game Ideas

Bring your handicap, bring your wallet.

🐺 Wolf
Rotating partners each hole. The "wolf" picks a partner after watching tee shots, or goes lone wolf for double points.
📊 Stableford
Points per hole. Birdie = 3, par = 2, bogey = 1. Run it across all 5 rounds for trip champion.
🥁 Bingo Bango Bongo
3 points per hole: first on green, closest to pin when all on, first in cup. Rewards consistency over power.

The Courses

From the oldest course in the west to a top-10 in America.

Day 2 · 8:40 AM

Del Monte Golf Course

The oldest continuously operating course west of the Mississippi (est. 1897)

Designer
Charles Maud
Par
72
Yards
6,365

A walk through history. Mature oaks, tight tree-lined fairways, and small greens. Not long, but you have to think your way around. Perfect opener to shake off the travel.

Day 2 · 3:50 PM

The Hay

A Tiger Woods-designed par 3 jewel at Pebble Beach

Designer
Tiger Woods
Holes
9 Par 3s
Yards
66-106

Originally designed by Peter Hay in 1957, redesigned by Tiger in 2021. Ocean views on every hole. Wedges and putter, that's it. Bring closest-to-pin money.

Day 3 · 7:40 AM

Pacific Grove Golf Links

"The Poor Man's Pebble Beach"

Designer
Chandler Egan
Par
70
Yards
5,727

Front nine is a tree-lined parkland. Back nine drops you onto windswept dunes on Asilomar State Beach with views of Point Pinos Lighthouse. The back 9 alone is worth the trip. Bring extra balls.

Day 3 · 2:20 PM · MAIN EVENT

Spyglass Hill

Top 10 course in America. Robert Trent Jones Sr. at his finest.

Designer
RTJ Sr.
Par
72
Yards
6,960

The crown jewel of the trip. First five holes are seaside dunes (Treasure Island), then it ducks into the Del Monte Forest for some of the most demanding golf you'll ever play. Plays the AT&T Pro-Am every year. Anything before 3:30 PM is guaranteed to finish before darkness.

Day 4 · 8:20 AM

Black Horse

Bayonet & Black Horse · Built by the Army, perfected for play

Designer
Gen. McClure
Par
72
Yards
7,024

Originally built for the Army at Fort Ord. Sits on a bluff with panoramic Monterey Bay views from nearly every hole. Less crowded than the Pebble courses, but tee-to-green it stands up to any of them. Perfect closer.
